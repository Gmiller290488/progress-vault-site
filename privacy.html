Privacy policy

Last updated 9 November 2026

The short version
  • Your photos are stored only on your iPhone, inside the app. We never upload them and can't see them.
  • There's no account, no cloud service and no analytics.
  • The free version shows adverts from Google AdMob. Google may collect some device data to show them, with your consent where the law requires it. Premium users don't get adverts.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Who we are

Progress Vault is an iPhone app made by Gareth Miller ("we", "us"). If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at gmiller290488@gmail.com.

Information that stays on your device

Everything you create in Progress Vault is stored on your iPhone, inside the app's private storage. None of it is sent to us.

  • Your photos, saved as files that iOS encrypts while your phone is locked. They don't appear in your Photos library.
  • Each photo's date, and any label you add.
  • Your settings, such as whether previews are hidden, whether the app lock is on, and your camera, timer and overlay preferences.
  • A small counter of how many free alignment-overlay photos you've used, and when you last used one. This is kept in the iPhone's keychain so it isn't reset by reinstalling the app. It contains only a number and a date.

Backups. If you back up your iPhone with iCloud Backup or to a computer, iOS includes the app's data, including your photos, in that backup. Backups are controlled by your device settings and Apple's terms, not by us.

Permissions the app asks for

Camera

Used only to take progress photos inside the app. The app doesn't record video or audio, and doesn't add your location to photos.

Face ID

If you turn on the app lock, iOS checks your face or passcode. The app is only told whether the check succeeded. It never receives your face data, which stays in your iPhone's Secure Enclave.

Advertising (free version)

The free version of Progress Vault shows full-screen adverts from Google AdMob, for example when you watch an advert to take an extra photo in a day. To show adverts and measure them, Google's software may collect information such as:

  • your IP address and the approximate location it indicates;
  • device and app information (model, operating system version, language, app version);
  • interactions with adverts (impressions and taps);
  • an identifier for the app on your device, used for frequency capping, fraud prevention and measurement.

Progress Vault doesn't ask for permission to track you across other companies' apps, so your device's advertising identifier isn't shared.

Your consent. In the UK, the European Economic Area and Switzerland, the app shows Google's consent form before any adverts are requested. You can choose whether Google may use your data for personalised adverts. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings → Privacy → Ad privacy choices.

Premium. If you have Premium, the app doesn't request adverts.

Google's use of this information is covered by its own policies: how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Purchases

Premium is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment. The app only receives confirmation of which purchase is active, so it can unlock Premium. We never see your name, Apple Account or payment details.

What we don't do

  • We don't have accounts or servers that store your data.
  • We don't use analytics or tracking tools in the app.
  • We don't sell or share your personal information.

If you've chosen to share crash reports and usage data with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may pass us anonymous crash reports. These don't include your photos.

Deleting your data

You can delete any photo in the app by swiping it in the list or using Edit. Deleting the app removes all of its photos and settings from your iPhone, apart from the small free-overlay counter described above. Copies in existing device backups follow your backup settings.

Children

Progress Vault isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from children.

Your rights

Because we don't collect or store personal data ourselves, there's usually nothing for us to access, correct or delete. Your data is on your device and under your control. For data Google collects through adverts, you can manage your choices in the app as described above, or contact Google. If you have any concerns, contact us and we'll help. In the UK you also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

Changes to this policy

If the way the app handles data changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
