Why not just hide them in Photos?

Built for progress photos, not holiday snaps

Your camera roll is the wrong place for photos you want to keep to yourself and compare day by day.

Off your camera roll

Photos stay inside the app. They never show up in your Photos library, Memories or shared albums.

Line up every shot

A faint overlay of your last photo helps you match your pose and framing, so changes are easy to see.

Locked with Face ID

Turn on the app lock and nobody gets in without your face or passcode, even if your phone is unlocked.

Lighter on storage

Each photo is saved exactly as the camera took it, in full quality, without the Live Photo video clip that Photos usually keeps.

Alignment overlay

See real change, not a different angle

Progress is hard to spot when every photo is framed differently. Progress Vault shows yesterday's photo faintly over the camera, so you can step into the same spot and pose before you take today's.

  • Adjust how strong the overlay is, or hide it
  • Self-timer (3 or 10 seconds) for hands-free shots
  • Remembers your front or back camera choice every day
