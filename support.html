Support

Answers to common questions. Can't find what you need? Get in touch below.

Your photos

Where are my photos stored?

Only on your iPhone, inside Progress Vault. They're not uploaded anywhere and don't appear in your Photos library, Memories or shared albums.

Will my photos move to a new iPhone?

Yes, if you set up your new iPhone by transferring directly from your old one, or by restoring an iCloud or computer backup of it. Progress Vault doesn't have its own cloud sync.

What happens if I delete the app?

Deleting Progress Vault permanently deletes its photos from your iPhone. They can only be recovered from a device backup made before you deleted the app.

Can I save photos to my Photos library or share them?

Not at the moment. Keeping photos out of your camera roll is the point of the app. If you'd like an export option, let us know.

How do I hide the thumbnails in the list?

Open Settings (the gear icon) and turn on Hide progress previews in list. Photos still open normally when you tap a day.

Taking photos

How many photos can I take each day?

The free version includes one photo a day, plus one extra by watching a short advert. Premium removes the limit.

How does the alignment overlay work on the free version?

Your first 7 photos with the overlay are free. After that you get one free overlay photo each week. Premium unlocks it every day. A free use only counts when you save a photo with the overlay showing.

The app says it can't use the camera.

Open the iPhone Settings app, scroll down to Progress Vault, and turn on Camera.

Face ID lock

How do I turn on the app lock?

In Progress Vault, open Settings and turn on Require Face ID (or Touch ID or passcode, depending on your iPhone). You'll be asked to confirm with Face ID first.

What if Face ID doesn't recognise me?

After a failed attempt, iOS offers to use your iPhone passcode instead. You can also tap Unlock with Face ID to try again. If you remove your iPhone's passcode, the app lock turns itself off, so you can never be locked out of your photos.

Premium and purchases

How do I restore Premium on a new iPhone?

Open Settings in Progress Vault and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy Premium.

How do I cancel my monthly subscription?

Open the iPhone Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Progress Vault. Apple handles all subscriptions, so we can't cancel or refund them for you. Apple can help with refunds at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I change my advert privacy choices?

If you're in the UK, EU or Switzerland, open Settings in Progress Vault and tap Ad privacy choices. Premium users don't see adverts at all.
